5. TELC B1 - EXACT STRUCTURE
======================================================================

Every one of the 20 TELC series contains three Teile.

TELC TEIL 1 - GLOBALVERSTEHEN

Content:
- One shared bank of seven headings labelled A-G.
- Five short texts labelled Text 1-Text 5.
- The student assigns one heading to each text.
- Five answers are submitted; two headings remain unused.

Desktop/laptop:
- Keep the exact Teil title and italic instruction at the top.
- Use a two-area layout where practical:
  - a sticky/shared heading bank on the left;
  - the five text cards with selectors on the right.
- Every text card must show the full text and one answer selector.
- The student must be able to compare all seven headings while answering all five texts.

Mobile:
- Use a stacked layout.
- Keep a persistent or easily accessible "Überschriften anzeigen" control.
- Open the heading bank in an accordion, bottom sheet, drawer or modal without navigating away and without losing answers.
- Do not use a narrow two-column layout that causes overflow.

Interaction rules:
- A heading may be assigned to only one text.
- Selecting a used heading should either move it safely after confirmation or show that it is already used.
- Changing an answer must release the previous heading.
- Display answered progress such as "3/5 beantwortet".
- Do not reveal whether a choice is correct before submission.

Expected content validation per series:
- 7 headings
- 5 texts
- 5 student answers

TELC TEIL 2 - DETAILVERSTEHEN

Content:
- One reading text.
- Five questions.
- Options a, b and c for each question.

Desktop/laptop:
- Source text on the left and questions on the right.
- Keep the source panel visible/sticky while the question panel scrolls when the existing layout permits.

Mobile:
- Show the source text first, then question cards.
- After the student scrolls beyond the source, provide a "Text anzeigen" or equivalent control that opens the exact source text in a drawer/modal/accordion.

Expected content validation per series:
- 1 source text
- 5 questions
- exactly 3 options per question

TELC TEIL 3 - SELEKTIVES VERSTEHEN

Content:
- Ten situations.
- Twenty advertisements labelled A-T.
- One advertisement is selected for each situation.
- Some advertisements remain unused.

Desktop/laptop:
- Situations and answer selectors in one panel.
- A visible/sticky advertisements bank in the other panel.
- Advertisement labels and texts must remain readable while answering.

Mobile:
- Use situation cards with a "Anzeige auswählen" control.
- Display the advertisement bank in a full-width drawer, modal, bottom sheet or expandable section.
- Allow previewing the full advertisement before confirming.

Interaction rules:
- Each advertisement is used at most once.
- Clearly mark advertisements already used.
- Do not rely exclusively on drag-and-drop. Dropdown/button selection must work with touch, mouse and keyboard.
- Display progress such as "7/10 beantwortet".

Expected content validation per series:
- 10 situations
- 20 advertisements
- 10 student answers
