# Partner Verification Flow

1. Register for an account.
2. Verify your email address.
3. Log in to your account.
4. Open **Profile** and activate the Partner Programme.
5. Your unique partner code and referral link are generated automatically.
6. Share your referral link with others.
7. When someone registers using your referral link and becomes eligible, the referral is recorded.
8. When an eligible referred user completes a verified subscription payment, the commission is added to your partner account.
9. Wait until the commission's holding period is completed.
10. Once the commission becomes available, request a withdrawal from your partner dashboard.
11. Track the payout status from your partner dashboard.
